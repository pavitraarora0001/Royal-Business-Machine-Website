## 2. Proprietary Products & Specialized SaaS Solutions

### 1. **NyayaSetu (Bridge to Justice)**
- **Concept:** AI-powered, SaaS-based e-governance platform connecting citizens with law enforcement agencies.
- **Core Capabilities:** Guided legal awareness for incident reporting; AI-assisted mapping of incidents to verified IPC/BNS sections; police dashboard with FIR drafting assistance; human-in-the-loop manual override with complete explainability.
- **Target Market:** State police departments, legal aid organizations, citizen e-governance portals.

### 2. **Dairy Muneem**
- **Concept:** End-to-end enterprise mobile application and logistics software for dairy federations and milk procurement centers.
- **Core Capabilities:** Real-time fat/SNF milk pricing calculator, daily farmer ledger, automated payment disbursement tracking, route collection tracking, and SMS receipt dispatch.
- **Commercial Value:** ₹2,50,000 deployment contract with regional dairy operators.

### 3. **HMS (Hospital Management Software)**
- **Concept:** Complete digital healthcare management platform for multi-specialty clinics and regional hospitals.
- **Core Capabilities:** Outpatient/Inpatient registration, doctor scheduling, electronic health records (EHR), automated pharmacy billing, lab diagnostic reporting, and insurance claims.
- **Commercial Value:** ₹3,50,000 enterprise deployment.

### 4. **Anti-Paper Leak Examination Integrity Software**
- **Concept:** Multi-layered security and biometric examination delivery system preventing confidential paper compromise.
- **Core Capabilities:** Dynamic cryptographic watermarking per candidate, GPS-locked question paper distribution, biometric candidate verification, and tamper-evident audit trails.

### 5. **TaxSetu**
- **Concept:** Automated GST, tax filing, and fiscal compliance platform designed for Indian MSMEs and trading businesses.
- **Core Capabilities:** Automated invoice scanning, reconciliation of GSTR-2B with purchase registers, automated mismatch alerts, and instant tax liability computation.

### 6. **MatraSense AI / Data Engine**
- **Concept:** Multilingual text and speech analytics engine engineered for Indian regional languages and dialectal variations.
- **Core Capabilities:** Voice-driven search for rural users, automated vernacular transcription, and customer sentiment analytics.
