## 1. On-Page SEO Architecture

1. **Title Tag Hierarchy:** Every page features a unique, keyword-rich `<title>` structured as `Page Name | Royal Business Machine`.
2. **Meta Descriptions:** Tailored descriptions (150–160 characters) providing clear value propositions, service summaries, and contact details without keyword stuffing.
3. **Canonical URLs:** Self-referencing canonical URLs defined across all 23 pages, preventing duplicate content dilution.
4. **Heading Structures:** Strict semantic heading hierarchy (`<h1>` for primary page theme, `<h2>` for major sections, `<h3>` for cards/modules) with zero duplicate H1 tags per view.
5. **Open Graph Protocol & Twitter Cards:** Full Open Graph (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`, `og:site_name`, `og:type`) and Twitter Cards (`twitter:card`, `twitter:site`, `twitter:creator`, `twitter:image`) linking to the high-resolution corporate logo emblem (`/assets/logo/logo.png`).
6. **Structured Data (JSON-LD):**
   - **Organization Schema:** Name, alternateName ("RBM"), founder ("Pavitra Arora"), foundingDate ("2019"), contact points (sales & support phone lines), social profiles.
   - **LocalBusiness Schema:** Address (Bharatpur, Rajasthan, 321001), telephone, email, geo coordinates, opening hours, price range.
7. **XML Sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`):** Automatically maps all 23 public URLs with change frequencies (`weekly`, `monthly`, `yearly`) and priority ratings (`1.0` to `0.4`).
8. **Robots Directives (`/robots.txt`):** Permits global crawling of public pages while shielding administrative portals (`/admin/`, `/client/`, `/api/`) and declaring sitemap location.
